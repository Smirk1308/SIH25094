Students completing higher secondary education in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics have access to diverse technical, defense, and analytical career pathways. Engineering remains the most chosen avenue, encompassing disciplines like computer science, civil, electrical, and mechanical engineering. Students interested in pure sciences can pursue Bachelor of Science degrees in mathematics, physics, data analytics, and statistics. Armed forces entry through defense services offers a prestigious pathway to serve as commissioned officers in the army, navy, or air force. Additionally, the merchant navy provides rewarding careers in nautical science and marine engineering, while architecture combines mathematics with creative spatial design through five-year degree programs.

Admission to premier engineering institutions is governed by the Joint Entrance Examination Main, conducted in two sessions typically held in late January and early April, followed by JEE Advanced in late May for Indian Institutes of Technology. Regional engineering seats across Jammu and Kashmir colleges are filled through the Jammu and Kashmir Common Entrance Test administered by BOPEE, generally scheduled between May and June. For architecture admissions, the National Aptitude Test in Architecture is conducted between April and July, alongside JEE Main Paper Two held in January and April. Defense aspirants appear for the National Defence Academy examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission twice a year in April and September. Candidates aiming for merchant navy careers take the Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test, held annually in May or June.

Students graduating with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology have rewarding opportunities in clinical healthcare, biomedical research, and allied therapeutic professions. Beyond the highly sought-after MBBS and Bachelor of Dental Surgery degrees, healthcare aspirants can pursue alternative medical systems such as the Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery. The nursing sector offers robust professional prospects through four-year Bachelor of Science in Nursing programs in government medical colleges and nursing institutes. Furthermore, paramedical and allied healthcare sciences, including medical laboratory technology, radiology, operation theatre technology, and physiotherapy, prepare vital healthcare personnel required across hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, and rehabilitation clinics.

The primary national gateway for undergraduate medical, dental, and AYUSH admissions is the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test, conducted annually by the National Testing Agency on the first Sunday of May. In Jammu and Kashmir, state-level counseling for MBBS, BDS, and BAMS seats is organized by the Board of Professional Entrance Examinations beginning around July or August. For admissions into Bachelor of Science Nursing and allied paramedical courses across government institutions in the region, BOPEE conducts the specialized nursing entrance test, typically held in May or June. Moreover, university admissions for pure biology, biotechnology, and agricultural science programs are processed through the Common University Entrance Test, scheduled every year in May.
