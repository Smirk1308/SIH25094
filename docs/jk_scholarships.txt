The Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme for Jammu and Kashmir students assists meritorious students from economically weaker backgrounds in pursuing post-secondary education. Students enrolled in government-approved diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional degree courses are eligible to apply. To qualify for financial assistance, the total annual family income of the candidate from all sources must not exceed Rupees 2.5 lakh. The application procedure is conducted digitally through the National Scholarship Portal at scholarships.gov.in. Applicants must complete registration, link their Aadhaar number, and upload their domicile certificate, income certificate, academic mark sheets, and bank account details. The annual application deadline is typically in the month of October. The scholarship covers approved institutional tuition fees along with a maintenance allowance deposited directly into the student bank account.

The Prime Minister Special Scholarship Scheme, commonly known as PMSSS, is a flagship central government initiative created to support youth from Jammu and Kashmir in pursuing higher education across India. Under this program, 5000 scholarship slots are allocated annually to students who have passed Class 12 from JKBOSE or CBSE schools in the region. The scheme comprehensively covers professional courses in engineering, nursing, pharmacy, and medical degrees such as MBBS and BDS. For medical admissions, qualifying NEET is mandatory, whereas engineering seats are allotted through centralized merit counseling. Candidates must apply exclusively through the official portal of the All India Council for Technical Education. The scholarship pays full academic fees directly to colleges and provides an annual maintenance allowance of Rupees 1 lakh for living and accommodation expenses.

The Merit-cum-Means Scholarship Scheme offered by the Ministry of Minority Affairs provides critical financial assistance to students from notified minority communities pursuing professional and technical courses. Students admitted to undergraduate or postgraduate technical degree programs in recognized colleges across India are eligible. To qualify, applicants must have secured at least 50 percent marks in their previous final examination. Additionally, the annual family income from all sources must be below Rupees 2.5 lakh. The scheme covers course fees up to Rupees 20,000 annually and provides a monthly maintenance allowance. Applications are submitted online through the National Scholarship Portal, followed by verification by the institution and district authorities.

The Inspire Scholarship, managed by the Department of Science and Technology, encourages students to pursue natural and basic sciences at university level. Students who have completed Class 12 with science subjects and scored above 60 percent marks in qualifying examinations, or ranked in top board percentiles, are eligible to apply. The program supports regular undergraduate and integrated master degree education in sciences such as physics, chemistry, mathematics, and biology. Selected scholars receive Rupees 80,000 annually, including educational support and a mentorship grant for summer research internships at premier laboratories.
