Government Medical College Srinagar and Government Medical College Jammu are the two premier and historic government medical institutions offering undergraduate medical education in the Union Territory. Established in the mid-twentieth century and recognized by the National Medical Commission, each college admits approximately one hundred eighty MBBS students each academic session. GMC Srinagar operates with major tertiary hospitals, including the Shri Maharaja Hari Singh Hospital and Lal Ded Hospital, providing comprehensive clinical exposure to students. Similarly, GMC Jammu is attached to associated hospitals in Jammu city, ensuring extensive hands-on patient training. Both institutions offer world-class clinical infrastructure, postgraduate programs across medical specializations, and state-subsidized tuition fees that make high-quality medical education affordable for local students.

Sher-i-Kashmir Institute of Medical Sciences, situated at Soura in Srinagar, stands as an autonomous deemed university and premier super-specialty healthcare and research institute in Jammu and Kashmir. Alongside the apex tertiary referral hospital at Soura, the affiliated SKIMS Medical College located at Bemina delivers undergraduate MBBS training with an annual intake of one hundred students. SKIMS is renowned for advanced medical diagnostics, specialized surgical departments, oncology care, and intensive clinical research. Medical students benefit from modern laboratories, extensive research grants, and training under distinguished medical specialists, establishing the institution as a focal center of healthcare excellence in northern India.

Admission to undergraduate MBBS and BDS courses across the region is strictly governed by the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test, with state counseling administered by the Jammu and Kashmir Board of Professional Entrance Examinations. Under the eighty-five percent state quota, competition for premier institutions is rigorous. In recent admission rounds, Open Merit closing scores for top choices like GMC Srinagar, GMC Jammu, and SKIMS Bemina have ranged between five hundred seventy and six hundred twenty marks out of seven hundred twenty. For newly established peripheral medical colleges like GMC Anantnag, Baramulla, and Kathua, Open Merit scores generally close around five hundred thirty to five hundred sixty marks. Reserved category cutoffs, including Residents of Backward Areas, Scheduled Castes, and Scheduled Tribes, typically range between four hundred twenty and five hundred ten marks.

Traditional and holistic medicine education in Jammu and Kashmir is offered through institutions operating under the Directorate of AYUSH and the Ministry of Ayush. The Government Ayurvedic Medical College located in Akhnoor, Jammu, provides the Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery degree, known as BAMS, with an annual intake of sixty students. In the Kashmir Valley, the Government Unani Medical College at Ganderbal provides Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery degrees. Admissions to these AYUSH programs are processed through NEET merit via BOPEE counseling rounds. Cutoff scores for BAMS programs generally range between three hundred fifty and four hundred seventy marks for Open Merit candidates, offering attractive medical career avenues in integrative and alternative healthcare systems.
