The Jammu and Kashmir Common Entrance Test, known as JKCET, is the primary regional entrance examination for undergraduate engineering admissions across colleges in Jammu and Kashmir. Conducted annually by the Board of Professional Entrance Examinations, the test is open to domiciles of the region who have passed Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, securing at least fifty percent aggregate marks. The online application window typically opens in March or April, with the examination conducted between May and June. In the medical domain, the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test serves as the unified national gateway for MBBS, BDS, and AYUSH courses. Administered by the National Testing Agency, candidates must have completed Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, attaining at least fifty percent marks and having reached seventeen years of age. NEET applications generally open in February and March, followed by the national examination held in May.

For legal education, the Common Law Admission Test, organized by the Consortium of National Law Universities, serves as the premier gateway for five-year integrated undergraduate law programs. To be eligible for undergraduate CLAT, applicants must have completed Class 12 with a minimum of forty-five percent marks for general categories and forty percent for reserved categories, with no upper age limit. The application cycle typically commences between July and August, running through November, with the examination held in December or May depending on academic notification cycles. Successful candidates secure admissions across national law universities for integrated degrees such as Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Laws.

The Jammu and Kashmir State Eligibility Test, known as JKSET, determines eligibility for assistant professorships in colleges and universities across Jammu, Kashmir, and Ladakh. Administered by nodal state universities, the examination is open to candidates holding a master degree with at least fifty-five percent marks, or fifty percent for reserved categories. The announcement and application timeline varies across academic sessions, with official notifications generally issued between April and July, followed by written testing in subsequent months.

State government recruitment is managed by the Jammu and Kashmir Public Service Commission and the Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board. JKPSC conducts the Combined Competitive Examination for executive civil service cadres, requiring a bachelor degree in any discipline, local domicile, and age between twenty-one and thirty-two years for open merit candidates. Application forms are generally invited during spring. Meanwhile, JKSSB conducts recruitment drives throughout the year for non-gazetted positions including junior assistants, account assistants, sub-inspectors, and revenue patwaris. Eligibility ranges from higher secondary certificates to graduation, with online registrations and tests scheduled across quarterly cycles.
