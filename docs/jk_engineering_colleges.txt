The National Institute of Technology Srinagar, situated at Hazratbal, is the premier institute of technical education in Jammu and Kashmir. As an Institute of National Importance, it offers four-year Bachelor of Technology degrees across disciplines including Computer Science and Engineering, Information Technology, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Chemical Engineering, and Metallurgical and Materials Engineering. Admissions are governed centrally through JEE Main scores via JoSAA counseling, featuring a fifty percent Home State quota for candidates from Jammu, Kashmir, and Ladakh. In recent admission cycles, the Home State closing rank for Computer Science has typically ranged between twenty-five thousand and thirty-five thousand. Information Technology and Electronics close around forty thousand to fifty-five thousand, while core branches like Civil, Mechanical, and Chemical Engineering usually close between eighty thousand and one lakh twenty thousand ranks for local candidates.

Islamic University of Science and Technology, situated in Awantipora, is a prominent public university providing quality engineering education in the Kashmir Valley. Its School of Engineering and Technology conducts undergraduate degree programs in Computer Science and Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Food Technology. Admissions are granted on the basis of JEE Main scores and university counseling merit lists. The fee structure at IUST is designed to remain accessible for regional students, with tuition and academic fees ranging between thirty-five thousand and forty-two thousand rupees per semester. This brings the total annual academic fee to approximately seventy thousand to eighty-four thousand rupees, excluding one-time registration and examination charges. Subsidized hostel accommodation is also provided for outstation students at nominal rates.

SSM College of Engineering, located at Parihaspora in Baramulla, is the oldest private engineering college established in the Kashmir Valley. Affiliated with the University of Kashmir and recognized by AICTE, the college offers undergraduate engineering courses in Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics and Communication Engineering. Candidates secure admission primarily through merit ranks achieved in the Jammu and Kashmir Common Entrance Test, known as JKCET, conducted annually by the Board of Professional Entrance Examinations. The institution also offers seats through institutional quota based on qualifying Class 12 board marks in physics, chemistry, and mathematics, providing an accessible engineering pathway for students across northern Kashmir.

Model Institute of Engineering and Technology, located at Kot Bhalwal in Jammu, is a leading autonomous engineering institution accredited with an A grade by NAAC. The institute offers degree programs in Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Civil Engineering. Admission to technical seats is administered through JKCET counseling organized by BOPEE, alongside allocations based on national JEE Main percentiles and higher secondary scores. The college maintains strong industry linkages, research incubation cells, and campus placement partnerships with major technology companies, making it a prominent destination for engineering aspirants in the Jammu region.
