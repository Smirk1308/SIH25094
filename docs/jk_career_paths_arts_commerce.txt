Higher secondary education in the arts and humanities stream unlocks foundational disciplines and dynamic professional career avenues for students. Candidates can pursue Bachelor of Arts degree programs specializing in subjects such as political science, history, economics, sociology, and psychology across regional government colleges and central universities. For legal careers, the Common Law Admission Test, widely known as CLAT, serves as the premier national entrance examination for admission into prestigious National Law Universities offering five-year integrated degree programs like Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Laws. Furthermore, creative students can pursue degree courses in journalism and mass communication, preparing for competitive careers in investigative reporting, broadcast media, digital journalism, public relations, and corporate communications.

Arts graduates frequently gravitate toward public policy and civil services, benefiting from deep curriculum alignment with general studies, governance, and administrative systems. Aspirants prepare for the Civil Services Examination conducted annually by the Union Public Service Commission to enter prestigious central administrative cadres such as the Indian Administrative Service, Indian Police Service, and Indian Foreign Service. At the regional level, the Jammu and Kashmir Public Service Commission conducts the Combined Competitive Examination for executive civil service postings, including the Junior Scale Administrative Service, Police Service, and Accounts Service. Both national and state civil service exams involve a multi-tier selection model consisting of preliminary screening tests, detailed written mains examinations, and comprehensive personality interviews.

The commerce stream provides essential training in financial accounting, commercial law, organizational economics, auditing, and corporate finance. Students generally enroll in three-year Bachelor of Commerce or Bachelor of Business Administration degree programs to establish fundamental corporate, analytical, and marketing proficiencies. For accounting aspirants, the Chartered Accountancy pathway administered by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India offers one of the most prestigious professional credentials. Students can register for the CA Foundation examination immediately after Class 12, advancing subsequently through the Intermediate course, three years of practical articleship training under a certified chartered accountant, and the rigorous CA Final examination.

Graduates across commerce and arts streams access diverse recruitment opportunities in banking, finance, and public administration throughout Jammu and Kashmir. The Jammu and Kashmir Bank periodically conducts competitive entrance examinations for recruitment of Probationary Officers and Banking Associates, serving as a primary banking employer for local graduates alongside national public sector bank exams organized by IBPS. Additionally, the Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board conducts recruitment drives for hundreds of non-gazetted government posts, including junior assistants, account assistants, sub-inspectors, and revenue patwaris. Alongside gazetted administrative recruitment by the Jammu and Kashmir Public Service Commission, these avenues provide stable and honorable public employment for dedicated graduates.
